AlbumLux privacy policy

AlbumLux is a Photoshop plugin made by Ovais Hamdani.

What the plugin keeps on your computer

  • Your settings, layouts, project files and design data.
  • Your Google email and the answer "paid or not, checked on this date".
  • An error log of the last 100 events, with no photos, no file paths and no email in it.

What goes over the internet

  • When you log in, Google tells our login service (Supabase) your email address. The plugin then asks "is this email a paid account?".
  • The update check reads a small public file with the newest version number.

That is all. Your photos, documents and designs never leave your computer. The login itself is kept only while the panel is open.

What we store

Only your Google email and your paid status. We do not sell or share it. We use Google sign-in only to read your email address.

Removing your data

In the plugin: Account > Log Out, then delete the "albumlux" folder from the Plug-ins folder of Photoshop. To remove your email from the paid list, message us.
